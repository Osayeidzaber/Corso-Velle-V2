Direction A · working name “Cut & Label” · proposal, not owner-approved

Campaign scale.
Garment-label precision.

Big, unboxed campaign photography meets the calm, exact language of a care label. Shopping surfaces stay light and quiet; every size, price and availability fact is set in a mono “spec” voice so it reads as information, not decoration. Motion is a short, decisive cut — never a float.

Spec voice — signature device
HEAVY JERSEY TEE[PRICE]
XSSMLXL· 3 COLOURS
Used on cards (size run), PDP (fit + measurements), cart lines (chosen options) and announcements. Strikethrough is always paired with text (“M — sold out”) for assistive tech and non-colour cues.
