# Form 4 — Products, operations, integrations, and store management

## Form title

Your clothing store — products and day-to-day operations

## Form description to paste

These answers help us make the website match how your business actually works. Estimates and Not sure are welcome. Where possible, distinguish what is true at launch from what you want later. Tell us the business rule rather than worrying about how to implement it. Share public links or a restricted project folder as appropriate; do not enter account passwords, authentication codes, private keys, card details, or individual customer/order records.

## Builder notes

R = required; O = optional. Optional routing: make O01 its own opening section. Existing website answers go to the migration section O02–O08; new-store answers skip to the catalog section O09. At the end of migration, continue to catalog. All other sections are sequential, with optional follow-ups. Do not use a checkbox question to route sections.
