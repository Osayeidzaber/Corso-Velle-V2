# Form 3 — The shopping experience

## Form title

Your clothing store — how customers browse and buy

## Form description to paste

Help us shape the actual shopping journey: finding products, comparing them, choosing a size, adding to the cart, and getting help. Describe what should happen, even if you do not know the technical name. Your answers guide the design; features will be checked against Shopify support, content readiness, usability, and the agreed scope.

## Builder notes

R = required; O = optional. Keep the small preference grids optional and provide Not sure choices. A preference is not automatically a launch requirement: the final form asks the owner to prioritize. Where a question says If relevant, keep it optional rather than trying to branch from a checkbox answer.
