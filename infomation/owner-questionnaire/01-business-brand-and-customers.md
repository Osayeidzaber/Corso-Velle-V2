# Form 1 — Your business, brand, and customers

## Form title

Your clothing store — business and brand discovery

## Form description to paste

Help us understand your brand, your customers, and what this website needs to achieve. There are no right design answers. Specific examples are more useful than words such as premium or modern on their own. Use Not sure where you need our recommendation. Estimates are fine; please label them as estimates. You can complete the other forms separately. Do not include passwords, verification codes, payment-card details, or customer records.

## Builder notes

Use the question ID at the start of each question title. R means required; O means optional. Choice lists are suggestions to paste into the question. Add the standard Other option where indicated. Show every question in this form; optional questions can be left blank. Current-store questions have Not applicable choices so a new brand can answer easily.
