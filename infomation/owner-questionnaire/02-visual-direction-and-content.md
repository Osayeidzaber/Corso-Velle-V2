# Form 2 — Visual direction, references, animation, and content

## Form title

Your clothing store — look, feel, and content

## Form description to paste

Tell us what you want customers to see and feel. Please open examples on your phone as well as a computer when possible. For a reference, explain the exact part you like; liking a whole website does not mean we should copy every detail. Screenshots, screen recordings, and public links are welcome through a shared folder. If unsure, choose Recommend and explain the outcome you want.

## Builder notes

R = required; O = optional. These are the owner's preferences, not approval to copy a reference's branding, images, or source. Keep visual questions neutral: there is no preselected house style. For V05–V07, put the public reference link in each question description. For V42, use a multiple-choice grid only if it is comfortable on the owner's phone; otherwise split into ordinary questions.
